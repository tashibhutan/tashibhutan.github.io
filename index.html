01 / About me

Research rooted
in the landscape.

I study how wildlife persists in complex, human-shaped landscapes—and how science can help it thrive.

My work combines field surveys, camera trapping, spatial ecology, and conservation genetics. I collaborate with government, communities, and conservation organisations to turn robust evidence into practical action for threatened species.
